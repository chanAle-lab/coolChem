# Topic 3.1.2 · Amount of Substance

**Specification Coverage:** Avogadro constant, mole calculations, ideal gas equation, empirical/molecular formulas, atom economy, percentage yield, titrations

## The Mole & Key Calculation Formulas

- **Avogadro constant (L or N_A):** 6.022 * 10^23 mol^-1 (number of entities in 1 mole of substance).
- **Mass formula:** n = m / M_r (m in grams, M_r in g mol^-1).
- **Solution formula:** n = c * V (c in mol dm^-3, V in dm^3; divide cm^3 by 1000).
- **Mass concentration:** conc (g dm^-3) = conc (mol dm^-3) * M_r.

## Ideal Gas Equation (pV = nRT)

- **Equation:** pV = nRT where R = 8.31 J K^-1 mol^-1.
- **SI Unit conversions (essential):**
- • Pressure p: in **Pa** (kPa * 1000 = Pa; MPa * 10^6 = Pa).
- • Volume V: in **m^3** (dm^3 * 10^-3 = m^3; cm^3 * 10^-6 = m^3).
- • Temperature T: in **Kelvin (K)** (°C + 273).
- **Finding Mr of volatile liquid:** M_r = (m * R * T) / (p * V).

## Empirical Formula, Yield & Atom Economy

- **Empirical formula:** Simplest whole-number ratio of atoms of each element in a compound. Calculate moles (mass / Ar), divide by smallest mole value to find ratio.
- **Molecular formula:** Actual number of atoms of each element. Scale factor = M_r / empirical mass.
- **Percentage yield:** (Actual yield / Theoretical yield) * 100%. (Reduced by incomplete reaction, reversible equilibria, side reactions, purification losses).
- **Percentage atom economy:** (M_r of desired product / Total M_r of all reactants) * 100%. Industrial importance: minimizes waste, conserves raw materials, lowers cost.
- **Titrations & Concordancy:** Concordant titres are within +/-0.10 cm^3 of each other. Only average concordant titres. Standard volumetric uncertainty calculation: percentage uncertainty = (uncertainty / reading) * 100%.
