# Topic 3.1.10 · Equilibrium Constant Kp (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Mole fractions, partial pressures, expressions for Kp, calculation of Kp, effect of temperature and pressure

## Mole Fractions & Partial Pressures

- **Mole fraction (x_A):** x_A = (moles of gas A) / (total moles of all gases). Sum of mole fractions = 1.0.
- **Partial pressure (p_A):** p_A = x_A * P_total.
- **Total pressure:** P_total = SUM p_i (Dalton's Law).

## Kp Expressions & Temperature Effect

- For aA(g) + bB(g) <=> cC(g) + dD(g):
- **Kp expression:** Kp = (pC^c * pD^d) / (pA^a * pB^b). Use round brackets and partial pressures, NOT square concentration brackets!
- **Kp Units:** Substitute pressure unit (kPa or Pa) and cancel.
- **Key principles:**
- • **ONLY temperature changes Kp**.
- • Changing pressure causes the system to adjust partial pressures so the quotient returns to the constant Kp value at that temperature.
- • Adding a catalyst has NO effect on Kp or position of equilibrium.
