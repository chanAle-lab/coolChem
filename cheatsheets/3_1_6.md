# Topic 3.1.6 · Chemical Equilibria & Kc

**Specification Coverage:** Dynamic equilibrium, Le Chatelier's principle, industrial compromises, equilibrium constant Kc

## Dynamic Equilibrium & Le Chatelier's Principle

- **Dynamic equilibrium conditions:** Closed system; forward and reverse reactions proceed at the exact same rate; concentrations of reactants and products remain constant.
- **Le Chatelier's Principle:** If a system at equilibrium is subjected to a change in conditions, the position of equilibrium shifts in the direction that opposes that change.
- • *Temperature increase:* Shifts in the endothermic direction (deltaH > 0) to absorb heat.
- • *Temperature decrease:* Shifts in the exothermic direction (deltaH < 0) to release heat.
- • *Pressure increase:* Shifts to the side with fewer moles of gas to reduce pressure.
- • *Catalyst:* Increases rates of forward and reverse reactions equally. **Does NOT change equilibrium position or yield**; reaches equilibrium faster.

## Equilibrium Constant (Kc)

- For homogeneous reaction aA + bB <=> cC + dD:
- **Kc expression:** Kc = ([C]^c [D]^d) / ([A]^a [B]^b) where square brackets denote equilibrium concentration in mol dm^-3.
- **Units:** Work out by substituting (mol dm^-3) into expression and cancelling out powers.
- **Crucial rule:** **ONLY TEMPERATURE alters the value of Kc**. Changes in concentration, pressure, or adding a catalyst do NOT alter Kc.
- • For exothermic reaction: increasing T shifts equilibrium left -> Kc decreases.
- • For endothermic reaction: increasing T shifts equilibrium right -> Kc increases.
