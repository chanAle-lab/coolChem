# Topic 3.1.11 · Electrode Potentials & Cells (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Standard hydrogen electrode, IUPAC cell conventions, EMF calculations, commercial cells, fuel cells

## Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE) & Cell Conventions

- **Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE):** Reference electrode assigned 0.00 V under standard conditions: 298 K, 100 kPa H_2 gas, 1.00 mol dm^-3 H^+ ions, platinised Pt electrode (inert catalyst).
- **IUPAC Cell Convention:**
- • Single line | indicates phase boundary (e.g. solid to aqueous).
- • Double line || indicates salt bridge (filter paper soaked in saturated KNO_3 or KCl).
- • Left electrode is oxidation (more negative E°); Right electrode is reduction (more positive E°).
- • Example: **Zn(s) | Zn^2+(aq) || Cu^2+(aq) | Cu(s)**.
- **EMF Calculation:** **E°_cell = E°_right - E°_left**. Reaction is feasible if E°_cell > 0.

## Commercial Cells & Hydrogen Fuel Cells

- **Lithium-ion secondary (rechargeable) cell:**
- • Positive electrode: Li^+ + CoO_2 + e^- -> Li^+[CoO_2]^-.
- • Negative electrode: Li -> Li^+ + e^-.
- **Alkaline Hydrogen-Oxygen Fuel Cell:**
- • Negative electrode (anode): **2H_2 + 4OH^- -> 4H_2O + 4e^-**.
- • Positive electrode (cathode): **O_2 + 2H_2O + 4e^- -> 4OH^-**.
- • Overall: **2H_2 + O_2 -> 2H_2O** (E° = +1.23 V).
- **Advantages:** Only water produced (zero emissions at point of use), higher energy efficiency than combustion.
- **Disadvantages:** Hydrogen storage under high pressure, hydrogen currently made from fossil methane.
