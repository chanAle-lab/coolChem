# Topic 3.2.3 · Group 7(17), The Halogens

**Specification Coverage:** Trends in electronegativity, boiling point, oxidising and reducing abilities; halide tests with silver nitrate and ammonia; chlorine chemistry

## Trends in Physical Properties & Oxidising Power

- **Electronegativity:** Decreases down group (F > Cl > Br > I) due to increased shielding and atomic radius.
- **Boiling points:** Increase down group (F_2 gas < Cl_2 gas < Br_2 liquid < I_2 solid) due to stronger van der Waals forces between larger molecules.
- **Oxidising ability:** **DECREASES down group** (Cl_2 > Br_2 > I_2). Fluorine is the most powerful oxidiser.
- • Cl_2 displaces Br^- (orange solution formed) and I^- (brown solution formed).
- • Br_2 displaces I^- only.
- • I_2 displaces neither.

## Reducing Ability of Halides with Conc H2SO4

- **Reducing power:** **INCREASES down group: F^- < Cl^- < Br^- < I^-** (larger ionic radius, electron more easily lost).
- • **NaCl + H_2SO_4:** Acid-base only: **NaCl + H_2SO_4 -> NaHSO_4 + HCl** (steamy fumes of HCl). No redox.
- • **NaBr + H_2SO_4:** Redox occurs: forms **orange fumes of Br_2** and choking toxic gas **SO_2** (sulfur reduced from +6 to +4).
- • **NaI + H_2SO_4:** Powerful reduction: forms **purple fumes / black solid of I_2**, choking **SO_2**, yellow solid sulfur **S** (+6 to 0), and rotten-egg gas **H_2S** (+6 to -2).

## Halide Ion Testing & Disproportionation of Chlorine

- **Testing for Halides:** Acidify with dilute **HNO_3** (removes CO_3^2- or OH^- which would form false ppts), then add **AgNO_3(aq)**:
- • **Chloride (Cl^-):** White precipitate (AgCl). Dissolves in **dilute NH_3**.
- • **Bromide (Br^-):** Cream precipitate (AgBr). Insoluble in dilute NH_3; dissolves in **concentrated NH_3**.
- • **Iodide (I^-):** Yellow precipitate (AgI). **Insoluble in concentrated NH_3**.
- **Disproportionation of Chlorine:**
- • *With water (water treatment):* **Cl_2 + H_2O <=> HClO + HCl**. Chloric(I) acid (HClO) sterilises drinking water. Health benefits far outweigh toxic risks.
- • *In sunlight:* 2Cl_2 + 2H_2O -> 4HCl + O_2.
- • *With cold dilute NaOH (bleach):* **Cl_2 + 2NaOH -> NaClO + NaCl + H_2O** (sodium chlorate(I) is active bleach).
