# Topic 3.3.2 · Alkanes

**Specification Coverage:** Fractional distillation of crude oil, cracking, combustion, pollution, free-radical chlorination mechanism

## Fractional Distillation & Cracking

- **Alkanes:** Saturated hydrocarbons with general formula C_nH_2n+2. Non-polar, held by weak van der Waals forces.
- **Fractional distillation:** Fractionating column has temperature gradient (hot at bottom, cool at top). Hydrocarbons vaporised; separate by boiling point (smaller molecules condense near top).
- **Thermal Cracking:** High temperature (700&ndash;1200 K) and high pressure (up to 7000 kPa). Produces high percentage of **alkenes** (used for polymers).
- **Catalytic Cracking:** Slight pressure, high temperature (720 K), **zeolite catalyst**. Produces branched alkanes, cycloalkanes, and aromatic hydrocarbons (used for motor fuels).

## Combustion & Free-Radical Substitution

- **Pollutants:** Complete combustion gives CO_2 and H_2O. Incomplete combustion gives toxic CO and carbon soot. High temperatures in internal combustion engines cause N_2 and O_2 to react forming NO_x.
- **Catalytic converters:** Platinum/rhodium catalyst: **2NO + 2CO -> N_2 + 2CO_2**.
- **Free-Radical Substitution of Methane (CH_4 + Cl_2):**
- • *Initiation:* Cl_2 -> 2Cl^* (homolytic fission triggered by UV light).
- • *Propagation 1:* Cl^* + CH_4 -> ^*CH_3 + HCl.
- • *Propagation 2:* ^*CH_3 + Cl_2 -> CH_3Cl + Cl^*.
- • *Termination:* ^*CH_3 + Cl^* -> CH_3Cl; ^*CH_3 + ^*CH_3 -> C_2H_6; Cl^* + Cl^* -> Cl_2.
