# Topic 3.1.8 · Thermodynamics (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Born-Haber cycles, lattice enthalpy, entropy change, Gibbs free energy, feasibility

## Born-Haber Cycles & Lattice Enthalpy

- **Lattice enthalpy of formation:** Enthalpy change when 1 mole of a solid ionic compound is formed from its constituent gaseous ions under standard conditions (always EXOTHERMIC, negative).
- **Lattice enthalpy of dissociation:** Enthalpy change when 1 mole of a solid ionic compound is completely separated into its gaseous ions (always ENDOTHERMIC, positive).
- **Cycle enthalpy steps:** Formation = Atomisation + Ionisation energies + Electron affinities + Lattice formation.
- **Electron affinity:** 1st EA is exothermic (negative) because incoming electron is attracted by nucleus; 2nd EA (e.g. O^- + e^- -> O^2-) is always **ENDOTHERMIC (positive)** due to strong repulsion between negative ion and incoming electron.
- **Born-Haber vs Perfect Ionic Model:** If experimental (Born-Haber) lattice enthalpy is significantly more exothermic than the theoretical value calculated assuming point charges (perfect ionic model), the compound has significant **covalent character** caused by cation polarisation.

## Entropy & Gibbs Free Energy

- **Entropy (S):** Measure of disorder of a system (in J K^-1 mol^-1). S(gas) >> S(liquid) > S(solid).
- **deltaS calculation:** deltaS = SUM S°(products) - SUM S°(reactants).
- **Gibbs Free Energy Equation:** **deltaG = deltaH - TdeltaS** (deltaH in kJ mol^-1, T in Kelvin, deltaS must be converted to kJ K^-1 mol^-1 by dividing by 1000!).
- **Feasibility:** Reaction is feasible when **deltaG &le; 0**.
- **Feasibility temperature:** T = deltaH / deltaS (at deltaG = 0).
- **deltaG vs T graph:** deltaG = -deltaS(T) + deltaH. Gradient = -deltaS, y-intercept = deltaH.
