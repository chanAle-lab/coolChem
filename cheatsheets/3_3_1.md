# Topic 3.3.1 · Introduction to Organic Chemistry

**Specification Coverage:** Nomenclature, formulas (empirical, molecular, structural, displayed, skeletal), curly arrow mechanisms, isomerism (structural, E/Z, CIP rules)

## Formulas & IUPAC Rules

- **Formulas:** Empirical (simplest ratio), Molecular (actual count), General (e.g. C_nH_2n+2), Structural (e.g. CH_3CH_2OH), Displayed (all bonds and atoms shown), Skeletal (carbon backbone zigzag, heteroatoms and functional groups explicitly shown).
- **IUPAC Nomenclature:** Identify longest continuous carbon chain (stem: meth, eth, prop, but, pent, hex); number chain from end giving lowest locator numbers to functional groups; list substituents alphabetically (ignoring di-, tri- prefixes).

## Reaction Mechanisms & Isomerism

- **Curly arrows:** Always represent the movement of an **electron pair**. Starts at a lone pair or covalent bond; points to an electron-deficient atom or the site where a new bond is formed.
- **Structural Isomerism:** Same molecular formula, different structural formula:
- • *Chain:* Different carbon skeleton arrangement.
- • *Position:* Same functional group at different positions on carbon skeleton.
- • *Functional group:* Different functional group (e.g. alkene vs cycloalkane, alcohol vs ether, aldehyde vs ketone, carboxylic acid vs ester).
- **Stereoisomerism:** Same structural formula, different arrangement of atoms in 3D space.
- **E/Z Isomerism:** Arises from restricted rotation about planar C=C double bond, and each C atom having two different groups attached. **Cahn-Ingold-Prelog (CIP) priority:** Higher atomic number gets higher priority on each double-bonded carbon. Z = high priority on same side (z-zame zide); E = high priority on opposite sides.
