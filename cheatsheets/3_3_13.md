# Topic 3.3.13 · Amino Acids, Proteins & DNA (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Amino acid zwitterions, peptide links, protein structure, enzymes, DNA structure and base pairing, cisplatin anticancer drug

## Amino Acids & Zwitterions

- **Structure:** 2-amino acids contain both -NH_2 and -COOH on the same carbon: H_2N-CHR-COOH.
- **Zwitterion:** Dipolar ion formed by internal acid-base transfer: ^+H_3N-CHR-COO^-. High melting points due to ionic attraction.
- • *In acidic solution (low pH):* -COO^- gains H^+ -> cation ^+H_3N-CHR-COOH.
- • *In alkaline solution (high pH):* ^+H_3N- loses H^+ -> anion H_2N-CHR-COO^-.

## Proteins, Enzymes, DNA & Cisplatin

- **Protein structure:** Primary (amino acid sequence), Secondary (&alpha;-helix and &beta;-pleated sheets stabilized by hydrogen bonds), Tertiary (3D folding stabilized by H-bonds, ionic bonds, and disulfide -S-S- bridges).
- **Enzymes:** Protein catalysts with **stereospecific active sites** that bind only one optical enantiomer of a substrate. Drugs act as competitive inhibitors.
- **DNA (Deoxyribonucleic acid):** Nucleotide = phosphate group + 2-deoxyribose + base (A, T, C, G). Two complementary anti-parallel strands in double helix held by **hydrogen bonds between base pairs: A pairs with T (2 H-bonds); C pairs with G (3 H-bonds)**.
- **Cisplatin:** Platinum(II) complex [Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]. Acts as anticancer drug: undergoes ligand substitution with N atom on guanine in DNA, kinking the DNA double helix and preventing replication. Side effects: hair loss and kidney damage.
