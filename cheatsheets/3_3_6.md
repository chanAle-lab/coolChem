# Topic 3.3.6 · Organic Analysis

**Specification Coverage:** Functional group test-tube reactions, high resolution mass spectrometry, infrared spectroscopy

## Chemical Tests for Functional Groups

- • **Alkene:** Shake with bromine water -> **Orange to colourless**.
- • **Halogenoalkane:** Warm with aqueous NaOH, acidify with HNO_3, add AgNO_3 -> AgCl white ppt, AgBr cream ppt, AgI yellow ppt.
- • **1° and 2° Alcohol:** Warm with acidified K_2Cr_2O_7 -> **Orange to green**.
- • **Aldehyde vs Ketone:**
-   - *Tollens' reagent ([Ag(NH_3)_2]^+):* Warm -> **Silver mirror** with aldehyde; no reaction with ketone.
-   - *Fehling's solution:* Warm -> **Blue solution to brick red precipitate (Cu_2O)** with aldehyde; stays blue with ketone.
- • **Carboxylic acid:** Add Na_2CO_3(s) or NaHCO_3(aq) -> **Effervescence of CO_2 gas** (turns limewater cloudy).

## High-Res Mass Spectrometry & Infrared (IR) Spectroscopy

- **High Resolution MS:** Measures molecular ion m/z to 4 or 5 decimal places. Enables distinguishing between molecules with identical integer masses (e.g. C_3H_6O = 58.0419 vs C_4H_10 = 58.0782) using precise isotopic masses.
- **Infrared Spectroscopy:** Covalent bonds vibrate and absorb specific IR frequencies corresponding to bond wavenumber.
- • **O-H (alcohols):** Broad absorption at **3230&ndash;3550 cm^-1**.
- • **O-H (carboxylic acids):** Very broad absorption overlapping C-H at **2500&ndash;3000 cm^-1**.
- • **C=O (carbonyl):** Strong sharp peak at **1680&ndash;1750 cm^-1**.
- • **C=C (alkene):** Peak at **1620&ndash;1680 cm^-1**.
- **Fingerprint region (<1500 cm^-1):** Unique pattern of complex bending vibrations; compare against computer library to identify exact molecule and check purity.
