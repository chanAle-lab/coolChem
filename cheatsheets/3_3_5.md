# Topic 3.3.5 · Alcohols

**Specification Coverage:** Industrial production (fermentation vs hydration), biofuels, oxidation of alcohols, elimination/dehydration

## Production of Ethanol & Biofuels

- **1. Hydration of Ethene:** CH_2=CH_2 + H_2O(g) <=> CH_3CH_2OH. Conditions: conc **H_3PO_4 catalyst**, 300°C, 60 atm. Continuous, pure product, fast, but uses non-renewable crude oil.
- **2. Fermentation of Glucose:** C_6H_12O_6 -> 2C_2H_5OH + 2CO_2. Conditions: yeast enzyme, 35°C, **anaerobic**. Batch process, slow, impure (needs fractional distillation), but uses renewable sugar cane/corn.
- **Biofuel & Carbon Neutrality:**
- • Photosynthesis: 6CO_2 + 6H_2O -> C_6H_12O_6 + 6O_2 (absorbs 6 CO_2).
- • Fermentation: C_6H_12O_6 -> 2C_2H_5OH + 2CO_2 (releases 2 CO_2).
- • Combustion: 2C_2H_5OH + 6O_2 -> 4CO_2 + 6H_2O (releases 4 CO_2).
- • Theoretically zero net CO_2, but in practice **not carbon neutral** due to fossil fuels burned in transport, farming machinery, fertiliser production, and distillation.

## Oxidation & Elimination of Alcohols

- **Oxidising agent:** Acidified potassium dichromate(VI) (K_2Cr_2O_7 / H_2SO_4). Orange Cr_2O_7^2- reduced to green Cr^3+.
- • **Primary alcohol:** Distil off immediately -> **Aldehyde**. Reflux with excess oxidiser -> **Carboxylic acid**.
- • **Secondary alcohol:** Reflux -> **Ketone**.
- • **Tertiary alcohol:** **Cannot be oxidised** (no hydrogen on the carbon bearing the -OH group; solution stays orange).
- **Elimination (Dehydration):** Heat with conc **H_2SO_4** or conc **H_3PO_4** at 170°C: Alcohol -> Alkene + H_2O. (Produces addition polymers without crude oil).
