# Topic RP · Required Practicals 1 to 12

**Specification Coverage:** The 12 AQA Required Practicals: apparatus, volumetric techniques, enthalpy, rates, tests for ions, distillation, organic synthesis, electrochemistry, pH curves, TLC

## Physical & Inorganic Practicals (RP1 – RP6)

- **RP1 (Titration):** Make volumetric solution in volumetric flask (invert to mix), rinse burette and pipette with reagents, concordant titres within 0.10 cm^3.
- **RP2 (Enthalpy):** Polystyrene cup calorimeter, measure initial temp for 3 mins, mix at min 4, record temp every min to min 10. Extrapolate cooling curve back to min 4 to find true deltaT. Calculate q = mcdeltaT.
- **RP3 (Rates & Temp):** Disappearing cross reaction (Na_2S_2O_3 + 2HCl -> 2NaCl + SO_2 + S + H_2O). 1/time is proportional to rate. Plot ln(1/t) vs 1/T for Arrhenius.
- **RP4 (Qualitative Analysis):** Test for Group 2 cations with NaOH/H_2SO_4; test for NH_4^+ with warm NaOH and damp red litmus (turns blue); test for halides with acidified AgNO_3; test for SO_4^2- with acidified BaCl_2; test for CO_3^2- with acid (CO_2 turns limewater cloudy).
- **RP5 (Distillation):** Distil ethanal from oxidation of ethanol using anti-bumping granules, thermometer bulb level with condenser side-arm, water in at bottom of condenser.
- **RP6 (Organic Tests):** Alkene (bromine water), 1°/2° alcohol (acidified dichromate), aldehyde (Tollens' silver mirror / Fehling's red ppt), carboxylic acid (Na_2CO_3 effervescence).

## A-Level Advanced Practicals (RP7 – RP12)

- **RP7 (Rate determination):** Initial rate by clock reaction (e.g. iodine clock) or continuous monitoring (gas syringe volume vs time; tangent to curve gives rate).
- **RP8 (Electrochemical Cells):** Clean metal strips with emery paper, degrease, potassium nitrate salt bridge connecting half-cells, high resistance voltmeter.
- **RP9 (pH Curves):** Calibrate pH meter using standard buffer solutions (pH 4, 7, 10). Add base in small portions (0.2 cm^3 near equivalence point), plot pH vs volume.
- **RP10 (Organic Synthesis & Purification):**
- • *Liquid:* Reflux, separating funnel (wash with NaHCO_3 to remove acid, run off aqueous layer), dry with anhydrous CaCl_2/MgSO_4, redistil.
- • *Solid (Aspirin):* Dissolve in minimum volume of hot solvent, hot filtration, cool to crystallise, Büchner vacuum filtration, wash with ice-cold solvent, dry in oven. Test purity with melting point apparatus (sharp mp matching literature indicates purity; impurities lower and broaden mp range).
- **RP11 (Transition Metal Ions):** Test [Fe(H_2O)_6]^2+, [Cu(H_2O)_6]^2+, [Fe(H_2O)_6]^3+, [Al(H_2O)_6]^3+ with NaOH, NH_3, and Na_2CO_3.
- **RP12 (Thin-Layer Chromatography):** Spot mixture on pencil line above solvent level, develop in sealed chamber with solvent vapor, visualize with ninhydrin or UV light, calculate Rf values.
