# Topic 3.3.11 · Amines (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Preparation of amines, base properties, nucleophilic substitution with haloalkanes, cationic surfactants

## Preparation & Base Strength

- **Preparation:**
- • *From halogenoalkanes:* Warm with excess ethanolic NH_3 under pressure.
- • *Reduction of nitriles:* R-CN + 4[H] -> R-CH_2NH_2 using LiAlH_4 in dry ether or H_2 / Ni catalyst.
- • *Aromatic amines:* Reduce nitrobenzene using **tin (Sn) and concentrated HCl**, then add NaOH to release free phenylamine.
- **Base Strength (lone pair availability on N):**
- • **Secondary aliphatic > Primary aliphatic > Ammonia > Aromatic amine**.
- • Alkyl groups have positive inductive effect (+I), pushing electron density onto N, making lone pair more available to accept H^+.
- • In phenylamine, the N lone pair delocalises into the aromatic pi ring, drastically reducing availability to accept protons.

## Amines as Nucleophiles & Cationic Surfactants

- **Successive substitution:** 1° amine reacts with haloalkane -> 2° amine -> 3° amine -> **quaternary ammonium salt [R_4N]^+X^-**.
- **Cationic Surfactants:** Quaternary ammonium salts with a long hydrocarbon tail and positive head. Positive head attracts negative surfaces (hair, fabrics), tail points outward, reducing static friction. Used in fabric softeners and hair conditioners.
