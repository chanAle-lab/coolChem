# Topic 3.2.4 · Period 3 Elements & Their Oxides (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Reactions of Period 3 with O2, structures, melting points, reactions with water and pH, acidic/basic/amphoteric behavior

## Oxides Formation, Structures & Melting Points

- **Reactions with Oxygen:**
- • 2Na + &frac12;O_2 -> Na_2O (yellow flame, white solid)
- • Mg + &frac12;O_2 -> MgO (brilliant white flame, white solid)
- • 4Al + 3O_2 -> 2Al_2O_3 (bright white sparks)
- • Si + O_2 -> SiO_2 (slow reaction)
- • P_4 + 5O_2 -> P_4O_10 (vigorous white flame, dense white smoke)
- • S + O_2 -> SO_2 (blue flame, choking gas); 2SO_2 + O_2 <=> 2SO_3
- **Structure & mp:** Na_2O, MgO, Al_2O_3 have giant ionic lattices (high mp; MgO highest due to Mg^2+ and O^2- high charge density). SiO_2 is giant covalent (very high mp). P_4O_10, SO_2, SO_3 are simple molecular with weak van der Waals (low mp).

## Reactions with Water & Acid-Base Character

- **Reactions with Water:**
- • Na_2O(s) + H_2O(l) -> 2NaOH(aq) (strongly alkaline, pH ~14)
- • MgO(s) + H_2O(l) <=> Mg(OH)_2(aq) (sparingly soluble, pH ~9)
- • Al_2O_3 and SiO_2: Insoluble in water due to high lattice/covalent energy (pH 7).
- • P_4O_10(s) + 6H_2O(l) -> 4H_3PO_4(aq) (phosphoric(V) acid, pH 0&ndash;1)
- • SO_2(g) + H_2O(l) <=> H_2SO_3(aq) (sulfuric(IV) acid, pH 2&ndash;3)
- • SO_3(l) + H_2O(l) -> H_2SO_4(aq) (sulfuric(VI) acid, pH 0)
- **Amphoteric nature of Al_2O_3:**
- • Acts as base: Al_2O_3 + 6HCl -> 2AlCl_3 + 3H_2O
- • Acts as acid: Al_2O_3 + 2NaOH + 3H_2O -> 2Na[Al(OH)_4]
