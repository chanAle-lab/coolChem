# Topic 3.1.12 · Acids, Bases & Buffers (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Brønsted-Lowry theory, pH, Kw, weak acids Ka, titration curves, indicators, buffer solutions

## pH, Kw & Strong Acids/Bases

- **Br&oslash;nsted-Lowry:** Acid = proton donor; Base = proton acceptor.
- **pH definition:** **pH = -log_10[H^+]** -> [H^+] = 10^-pH. (Report pH to 2 decimal places!).
- **Strong monoprotic acid:** Fully dissociated, so [H^+] = [acid].
- **Ionic product of water (Kw):** Kw = [H^+][OH^-] = 1.00 * 10^-14 mol^2 dm^-6 at 298 K. Dissociation is endothermic, so Kw increases as T increases -> pure water pH < 7 at higher T (still neutral as [H^+] = [OH^-]).
- **Strong base calculation:** [H^+] = Kw / [OH^-] -> pH = -log_10[H^+].

## Weak Acids (Ka) & Titration Curves

- **Acid dissociation constant (Ka):** Ka = ([H^+][A^-]) / [HA]. pKa = -log_10Ka.
- **Weak acid approximation:** [H^+] = sqrt(Ka * c).
- **Half-neutralisation point:** When [HA] = [A^-], **pH = pKa**.
- **Indicators:** Change colour over pH range matching the vertical equivalence steep section:
- • *Strong acid - Strong base:* Vertical pH 3&ndash;9. Methyl orange or Phenolphthalein.
- • *Weak acid - Strong base:* Vertical pH 7&ndash;10. **Phenolphthalein** (colourless to pink).
- • *Strong acid - Weak base:* Vertical pH 3&ndash;7. **Methyl orange** (red to yellow).
- • *Weak acid - Weak base:* No sharp vertical break; use pH meter.

## Buffer Solutions

- **Definition:** A solution that resists changes in pH when small amounts of acid or alkali are added.
- **Acidic buffer:** Weak acid + salt of that weak acid (e.g. CH_3COOH and CH_3COONa).
- • *Adding H^+:* Reacts with salt anion: A^- + H^+ -> HA.
- • *Adding OH^-:* Reacts with weak acid: HA + OH^- -> A^- + H_2O.
- **Buffer calculation:** [H^+] = Ka * ([acid] / [salt]).
