# Topic 3.3.9 · Carboxylic Acids & Derivatives (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Esterification, uses of esters, triglycerides, soap, biodiesel, acylation mechanisms with acyl chlorides and anhydrides

## Esters, Fats & Biodiesel

- **Esterification:** Carboxylic acid + alcohol <=> Ester + water (conc H_2SO_4 catalyst, heat under reflux). Sweet smelling, used in perfumes, solvents, food flavourings.
- **Hydrolysis of esters:**
- • *Acid hydrolysis:* Reflux with dilute acid <=> Carboxylic acid + alcohol (reversible).
- • *Alkaline hydrolysis (saponification):* Reflux with aqueous NaOH -> **Carboxylate salt + alcohol** (irreversible, higher yield).
- **Vegetable oils & fats:** Triesters of propane-1,2,3-triol (glycerol) and long-chain fatty acids. Alkaline hydrolysis produces **soap** (sodium salts of fatty acids) and glycerol.
- **Biodiesel:** Mixture of methyl esters of fatty acids made by reacting vegetable oils with methanol using a KOH catalyst.

## Acylation (Nucleophilic Addition-Elimination)

- **Acylating agents:** Acyl chlorides (RCOCl) and Acid anhydrides ((RCO)_2O).
- **Reactions with nucleophiles:**
- • + H_2O -> Carboxylic acid + HCl / RCOOH (steamy fumes)
- • + Alcohol -> Ester + HCl / RCOOH
- • + NH_3 -> Primary amide + NH_4Cl / RCOONH_4
- • + Primary amine -> N-substituted amide + R'NH_3Cl
- **Manufacture of Aspirin:** Salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) + ethanoic anhydride -> Aspirin + ethanoic acid.
- **Why ethanoic anhydride is preferred industrially:** Cheaper; less corrosive; slower and less violent reaction; does not produce dangerous toxic fumes of HCl gas.
