# Topic 3.3.12 · Polymers (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Condensation polymers (polyesters, polyamides), repeating units, intermolecular forces, disposal & biodegradability

## Condensation Polymers

- **Condensation polymerisation:** Monomers join with the elimination of a small molecule (usually H_2O or HCl).
- **1. Polyesters:** Formed from dicarboxylic acid + diol (or hydroxycarboxylic acid).
- • *Terylene (PET):* Benzene-1,4-dicarboxylic acid + ethane-1,2-diol. Linkage: ester (-COO-). Used in clothing and drink bottles.
- **2. Polyamides:** Formed from dicarboxylic acid + diamine (or amino acid).
- • *Nylon 6,6:* Hexanedioic acid + hexane-1,6-diamine. Linkage: peptide/amide (-CONH-). Used in ropes and carpets.
- • *Kevlar:* Benzene-1,4-dicarboxylic acid + benzene-1,4-diamine. Strong rigid chains with **extensive intermolecular hydrogen bonding**; used in bulletproof vests and fire protection gear.

## Disposal & Biodegradability

- **Addition polymers (polyalkenes):** Non-polar, chemically inert C-C backbone. Non-biodegradable; persist in landfills for centuries.
- **Condensation polymers:** Contain polar ester and amide bonds susceptible to **hydrolysis by acid or alkali**; they are **biodegradable**.
- **Recycling:** Reduces landfill waste and conserves fossil fuels, but requires sorting and cleaning, which is labour intensive and costly.
