# Topic 3.3.8 · Aldehydes & Ketones (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Nucleophilic addition reactions with NaBH4 and KCN, hazards of cyanide, mechanism

## Nucleophilic Addition Mechanisms

- **Carbonyl group polarity:** C=delta+ and O=delta-. The planar carbon is susceptible to nucleophilic attack.
- **1. Reduction using NaBH_4 in aqueous solution:**
- • Nucleophile is hydride ion **:H^-**.
- • Aldehyde -> Primary alcohol; Ketone -> Secondary alcohol.
- • Mechanism: :H^- attacks C^delta+, C=O pi electron pair moves onto O; intermediate alkoxide attacks H^delta+ of H_2O.
- **2. Addition of KCN followed by dilute acid:**
- • Forms a **hydroxynitrile** (2-hydroxyalkanenitrile). Useful in organic synthesis because it increases chain length by one carbon.
- • Mechanism: :CN^- attacks planar C=O; O^- protonated by H^+.
- • **Formation of racemic mixture:** Planar C=O can be attacked by :CN^- from above or below the plane with equal probability, producing equal amounts of enantiomers.
- • **Cyanide hazards:** KCN is highly toxic. Acidification releases lethal HCN gas. Must use a fume cupboard.
