# Topic 3.3.16 · Chromatography (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Thin-layer chromatography (TLC), column chromatography (CC), gas chromatography (GC), Rf values, GC-MS

## Chromatographic Principles & Techniques

- **Separation mechanism:** Separation depends on the balance between solubility in the moving phase and retention/adsorption by the stationary phase.
- • **Thin-Layer Chromatography (TLC):** Stationary phase is solid silica or alumina plate; mobile phase is liquid solvent. **Retention factor: Rf = (distance by spot) / (distance by solvent front)**.
- • **Column Chromatography (CC):** Used to purify and separate preparative quantities of mixtures. Stationary phase packed into glass column; solvent washes through.
- • **Gas Chromatography (GC):** Stationary phase is liquid or solid coating in long capillary column; mobile phase is inert carrier gas (He, N_2). Substances identified by **retention time**. Area under peak is proportional to quantity.
- • **GC-MS:** GC separates components; MS immediately analyzes each to determine precise molecular mass and fragmentation pattern.
