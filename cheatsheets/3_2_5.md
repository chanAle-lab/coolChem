# Topic 3.2.5 · Transition Metals (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Transition metal characteristics, complexes, ligands, isomerism, origin of colour, variable oxidation states, catalysts

## Characteristics & Ligand Substitution

- **Definition:** A transition element forms at least one stable ion with a partially filled d sub-level (Sc and Zn are not transition metals).
- **Properties:** Variable oxidation states, complex formation, coloured ions, catalytic activity.
- **Ligands:** Monodentate (H_2O, NH_3, Cl^-), Bidentate (1,2-diaminoethane 'en', C_2O_4^2-), Multidentate (EDTA^4-, haem).
- **Chelate Effect:** Substitution of monodentate ligands by multidentate ligands produces more product particles than reactants -> **deltaS is positive** -> deltaG < 0, making the complex far more stable.
- **Shapes of complexes:** Octahedral (6-coord with small ligands H_2O, NH_3, 90°), Tetrahedral (4-coord with large Cl^-, 109.5° e.g. [CuCl_4]^2-), Square planar (cisplatin [Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]), Linear (Tollens' [Ag(NH_3)_2]^+).

## Coloured Ions & Catalysis

- **Origin of Colour:** Ligands cause 3d orbitals to split into two different energy levels. Electrons absorb specific frequencies of visible light (deltaE = hv = hc/lambda) to promote an electron from ground state to excited state. Transmitted/reflected light is the complementary colour.
- **Factors affecting colour (deltaE):** Central metal ion, oxidation state, coordination number, and ligand identity.
- **Catalysts:**
- • *Heterogeneous (Contact process):* V_2O_5 + SO_2 -> V_2O_4 + SO_3, then V_2O_4 + &frac12;O_2 -> V_2O_5.
- • *Homogeneous (S_2O_8^2- + 2I^-):* Catalysed by Fe^2+: S_2O_8^2- + 2Fe^2+ -> 2SO_4^2- + 2Fe^3+, then 2Fe^3+ + 2I^- -> 2Fe^2+ + I_2.
- • *Autocatalysis (MnO_4^- + C_2O_4^2-):* Mn^2+ ions produced catalyse the reaction.
