# Topic 3.2.6 · Reactions of Ions in Aqueous Solution (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Metal-aqua ions, acidity of 2+ vs 3+ ions, test-tube reactions with NaOH, NH3, and Na2CO3

## Acidity of Metal-Aqua Ions

- **Metal-aqua ions:** [Fe(H_2O)_6]^2+ (pale green), [Cu(H_2O)_6]^2+ (blue), [Fe(H_2O)_6]^3+ (purple/yellow-brown), [Al(H_2O)_6]^3+ (colourless).
- **Why 3+ ions are more acidic than 2+ ions:** 3+ ions have a higher charge and smaller ionic radius (greater charge/size ratio). They polarise the O-H bonds in coordinated water molecules more strongly, weakening the O-H bond and releasing H^+ into solution.

## Test-Tube Observations (Essential Summary Table)

- **[Fe(H_2O)_6]^2+:** +NaOH -> green ppt Fe(H_2O)_4(OH)_2 (turns brown in air). +excess NH_3 -> green ppt (insoluble in excess). +Na_2CO_3 -> green ppt of FeCO_3 (no gas).
- **[Cu(H_2O)_6]^2+:** +NaOH -> blue ppt Cu(H_2O)_4(OH)_2. +excess NH_3 -> **deep blue solution [Cu(NH_3)_4(H_2O)_2]^2+**. +Na_2CO_3 -> blue-green ppt of CuCO_3 (no gas).
- **[Fe(H_2O)_6]^3+:** +NaOH -> brown ppt Fe(H_2O)_3(OH)_3. +NH_3 -> brown ppt. +Na_2CO_3 -> **brown ppt AND effervescence of CO_2 gas** (3+ ion is acidic enough to liberate CO_2 from carbonate).
- **[Al(H_2O)_6]^3+:** +NaOH -> white ppt Al(H_2O)_3(OH)_3; **redissolves in excess NaOH to colourless [Al(OH)_4]^-** (amphoteric!). +NH_3 -> white ppt (insoluble in excess). +Na_2CO_3 -> **white ppt AND effervescence of CO_2 gas**.
