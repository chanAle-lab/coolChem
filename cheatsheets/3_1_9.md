# Topic 3.1.9 · Rate Equations & Arrhenius (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Rate equations, orders of reaction, rate constant, rate-determining step, Arrhenius equation

## Rate Equations & Orders of Reaction

- **Rate equation:** Rate = k[A]^m[B]^n where m and n are reaction orders (0, 1, or 2), and k is rate constant.
- • **Zero order (m = 0):** Concentration has NO effect on rate. Rate = k. [A]-time graph is linear downward slope.
- • **First order (m = 1):** Rate is directly proportional to [A]. Constant half-life t_&frac12;.
- • **Second order (m = 2):** Doubling [A] quadruples the rate (2^2 = 4).
- **Units of k:** Units = (mol dm^-3 s^-1) / [concentration units]^overall order. E.g. 1st order: s^-1; 2nd order: mol^-1 dm^3 s^-1; 3rd order: mol^-2 dm^6 s^-1.
- **Rate Determining Step (RDS):** The slowest step in a reaction mechanism. Species appearing in the rate equation must participate in the RDS or steps preceding it.

## The Arrhenius Equation

- **Equation:** k = A e^-E_a / RT.
- **Logarithmic form:** **ln k = -(E_a / R)(1/T) + ln A**.
- **Graphical analysis:** Plot ln k (y-axis) against 1/T (x-axis, in K^-1).
- • **Gradient = -E_a / R** -> E_a = -gradient * 8.31 J mol^-1 (divide by 1000 for kJ mol^-1).
- • **y-intercept = ln A** -> A = e^intercept.
