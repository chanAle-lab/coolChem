# Topic 3.3.3 · Halogenoalkanes

**Specification Coverage:** Nucleophilic substitution mechanisms, bond enthalpy vs polarity, elimination reactions, ozone depletion by CFCs

## Nucleophilic Substitution Mechanisms

- **Polar C-X bond:** Carbon has partial positive charge (delta+) attacked by electron-rich nucleophiles.
- • **Warm aqueous NaOH:** Nucleophile :OH^- replaces X to form **alcohols**.
- • **Ethanolic KCN:** Nucleophile :CN^- forms **nitriles** (extends carbon chain by one carbon atom).
- • **Excess ethanolic NH_3 under pressure:** Nucleophile :NH_3 forms **primary amines** (excess NH_3 prevents further substitution).
- **Reactivity order:** **R-I > R-Br > R-Cl > R-F**. The **C-I bond enthalpy is the lowest** and easiest to break, so iodoalkanes react fastest regardless of bond polarity.

## Elimination vs Substitution & Ozone Layer

- **Elimination:** Reagent: **hot ethanolic KOH**. The OH^- acts as a **base**, abstracting a proton from an adjacent carbon atom, forming an **alkene** + H_2O + X^-.
- **Condition comparison:** Aqueous + warm -> substitution. Ethanolic + hot reflux -> elimination.
- **Ozone Depletion by CFCs:**
- • UV photolysis: **CF_2Cl_2 -> ^*CF_2Cl + Cl^***
- • Catalytic destruction: **Cl^* + O_3 -> ClO^* + O_2** and **ClO^* + O_3 -> Cl^* + 2O_2** (overall 2O_3 -> 3O_2). Chlorine radicals are regenerated to destroy thousands of ozone molecules.
