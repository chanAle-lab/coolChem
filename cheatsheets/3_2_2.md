# Topic 3.2.2 · Group 2, Alkaline Earth Metals

**Specification Coverage:** Trends in atomic radius, first IE, melting point, reactivity with water; solubilities of hydroxides and sulfates; uses

## Group 2 Trends & Reactions with Water

- **Trends down Group 2 (Mg to Ba):** Atomic radius increases; first & second ionisation energies decrease; reactivity with water increases.
- **Reaction with water:**
- • *Mg with cold water:* Very slow: **Mg + 2H_2O(l) -> Mg(OH)_2 + H_2**.
- • *Mg with steam:* Rapid, white flame, white ash: **Mg(s) + H_2O(g) -> MgO(s) + H_2(g)**.
- • *Ca, Sr, Ba:* React with increasing vigour with cold water to form alkaline hydroxide solutions and effervescence of H_2.

## Solubility Trends & Real-World Uses

- **Hydroxides (M(OH)_2):** Solubility **INCREASES down the group**.
- • **Mg(OH)_2:** Sparingly soluble. Used in medicine as *Milk of Magnesia* (antacid for indigestion; OH^- neutralises stomach acid; safe because low solubility prevents harmful high pH).
- • **Ca(OH)_2:** Partially soluble (slaked lime). Used in agriculture to neutralise acidic soils.
- **Sulfates (MSO_4):** Solubility **DECREASES down the group**.
- • **BaSO_4:** Completely insoluble. Used as *Barium meal* in medicine for X-ray imaging of digestive tract (safe despite toxic Ba^2+ because its extreme insolubility prevents absorption into bloodstream).
- **Test for Sulfate ions:** Add dilute HCl (to remove interfering CO_3^2- ions), then add **BaCl_2(aq)**. Positive result: thick **white precipitate of BaSO_4**.
- **Extraction of Titanium:** Mg acts as reducing agent: **TiCl_4 + 2Mg -> Ti + 2MgCl_2** (high temp, argon atmosphere).
- **Flue Gas Desulfurisation:** CaO or CaCO_3 slurry sprayed into chimney emissions to react with acidic SO_2: **CaO + SO_2 -> CaSO_3**.
