# Topic 3.1.7 · Oxidation, Reduction & Redox Equations

**Specification Coverage:** Oxidation states, electron transfer, half-equations, combining redox equations

## Oxidation States & Redox Definitions

- **OIL RIG:** Oxidation Is Loss of electrons; Reduction Is Gain of electrons.
- **Oxidising agent:** Electron acceptor (gets reduced itself; causes oxidation).
- **Reducing agent:** Electron donor (gets oxidised itself; causes reduction).
- **Oxidation state rules:**
- • Uncombined elements = 0.
- • Neutral compounds sum to 0; polyatomic ions sum to overall ionic charge.
- • Group 1 metals = +1; Group 2 = +2; Aluminium = +3.
- • Fluorine is always -1.
- • Oxygen is -2 (except in peroxides H_2O_2 where it is -1, and in OF_2 where it is +2).
- • Hydrogen is +1 (except in metal hydrides NaH where it is -1).
- • Chlorine is -1 (except in compounds with F or O e.g. ClO^- where Cl is +1).

## Balancing Redox Half-Equations

- **Step-by-step method:**
- 1. Balance atoms other than H and O.
- 2. Balance oxygen atoms by adding H_2O molecules.
- 3. Balance hydrogen atoms by adding H^+ ions.
- 4. Balance electric charge by adding electrons (e^-).
- **Key half-equations:**
- • Manganate(VII) reduction: **MnO_4^- + 8H^+ + 5e^- -> Mn^2+ + 4H_2O**.
- • Dichromate(VI) reduction: **Cr_2O_7^2- + 14H^+ + 6e^- -> 2Cr^3+ + 7H_2O**.
- • Ethanedioate oxidation: **C_2O_4^2- -> 2CO_2 + 2e^-**.
- • Iron(II) oxidation: **Fe^2+ -> Fe^3+ + e^-**.
- • Hydrogen peroxide: **H_2O_2 -> O_2 + 2H^+ + 2e^-**.
