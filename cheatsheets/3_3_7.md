# Topic 3.3.7 · Optical Isomerism (A-Level)

**Specification Coverage:** Asymmetric carbon atom, enantiomers, plane-polarised light, racemates

## Chirality & Enantiomers

- **Chiral centre / Asymmetric carbon:** A carbon atom bonded to four different groups/atoms (marked with an asterisk *C).
- **Optical isomers (enantiomers):** Non-superimposable mirror images of each other.
- **Optical activity:** Each enantiomer rotates plane-polarised light by equal angles in opposite directions (+ / clockwise and - / anticlockwise).
- **Racemic mixture (racemate):** An equimolar (50:50) mixture of two enantiomers. It is **optically inactive** because the opposite rotations of each enantiomer cancel each other out.
- **Origin of racemates:** Formed when planar trigonal groups (e.g. C=O carbonyl or planar carbocation) are attacked by a nucleophile from either face with equal 50% probability.
